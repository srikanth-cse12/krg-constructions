ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Family-Owned Construction Materials Manufacturer & Supplier

Established in 2010, KRG CONSTRUCTIONS is an independent, family-owned business situated in Madagondapalli, Tamil Nadu.

We operate with a clear dual purpose: as a direct manufacturer of high-density solid cement bricks, and as a dependable supplier of essential civil construction materials including triple-washed M-Sand, P-Sand, stone dust, verified brand cement, and machine-crushed 20 mm / 40 mm blue metal jelly.

Over the past 14+ years, our family has built enduring relationships with homeowners, masons, and civil builders across Madagondapalli and surrounding regions. We focus squarely on dependable product quality, honest material measures, and attentive customer service on every single order.

SEMI-AUTOMATIC BRICK PLANT

How We Manufacture Solid Cement Bricks

KRG CONSTRUCTIONS manufactures 4-inch and 6-inch solid cement bricks using semi-automatic machinery, controlled hydraulic pressure, and systematic water curing in Madagondapalli.

100% Solid Cement Bricks Specification

Completely Solid Concrete Bodies — Never Hollow Blocks

Every cement brick manufactured at our plant is 100% completely solid throughout. We strictly do not manufacture or supply hollow blocks, perforated bricks, or bricks with core cavities. Our bricks are engineered with continuous dense concrete for maximum compressive strength, load-bearing durability, and acoustic insulation.

4-inch Solid Bricks
Standard 4" Width • Solid Body

Engineered for internal partition walls, compound boundary fencing, parapets, and residential room divisions.

6-inch Solid Bricks
Standard 6" Width • Solid Body

Heavy-duty dense solid bricks for exterior load-bearing walls, foundation plinths, and structural masonry.
